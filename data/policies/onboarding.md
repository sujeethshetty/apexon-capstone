# New Employee Onboarding Checklist

## Before the start date
Northstar Labs is a fictional company. The hiring manager submits the starter request in ServiceDesk at least 5 working days before the start date. Include role, team, start date, required applications, and the designated onboarding buddy. IT prepares a company-managed laptop and a standard account package.

## First day
The new employee meets their manager and onboarding buddy, collects their laptop, enrolls in multi-factor authentication, and verifies access to email and the knowledge portal. The manager explains team collaboration hours and the first-week plan.

## First week and first month
Complete security awareness training within 5 working days of starting. The manager confirms role-specific application access with each application owner. Complete the onboarding checklist in PeopleDesk by the end of the first 30 calendar days.

## Equipment allowance
The home-office allowance eligibility starts after 30 calendar days of employment. Refer to NS-HR-002 for the USD 300 allowance, eligible items, and approval requirements. The onboarding buddy may help with navigation but cannot approve access or expenses.
