# Customer Support Severity and Response Targets

## Intake and hours
Northstar Labs is a fictional company. Customers open support cases in the Atlas support portal. Standard staffed support hours are 09:00–17:00 UTC, Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays. Support response targets describe the first human response, not resolution or restoration time.

## Severity levels
Severity 1 means the production service is unavailable to all users with no viable workaround. The initial response target is 1 staffed support hour.
Severity 2 means a major function is unavailable to a subset of users and a workaround exists. The initial response target is 4 staffed support hours.
Severity 3 means a general question or minor issue that does not prevent normal work. The initial response target is 2 working days.

## Escalation and scope
Include the organization name, affected function, observed impact, start time, and safe diagnostic details in the case. Do not include passwords, API keys, or authentication tokens. If a case exceeds its first-response target, request escalation in the existing support case rather than creating duplicate cases. No 24/7 coverage, guaranteed resolution time, or contractual service-credit entitlement is specified here.
