# Atlas Product Plans and Data Export

## Product overview
Northstar Labs is a fictional company. Atlas is its fictional team knowledge workspace. Atlas has two demonstration plans: Core and Enterprise. Core includes shared workspaces, document search, and CSV export. Enterprise includes every Core feature plus SAML single sign-on, audit-log export, and configurable retention.

## Export behavior
Workspace administrators can export workspace records as CSV from Settings > Data Export. An export contains record text, record IDs, creation timestamps, and document titles. Uploaded binary attachments are not included in the CSV export. Only organization administrators can export audit logs, and audit-log export requires the Enterprise plan.

## Retention and pricing
Enterprise retention can be configured from 30 to 365 days. This demonstration document does not specify a default retention period. Product pricing, service availability commitments, and roadmap release dates are not published in this corpus.
