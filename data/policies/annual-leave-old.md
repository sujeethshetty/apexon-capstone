# Annual Leave Policy (Superseded)

## Archived policy: not current
Northstar Labs is a fictional company. This archived policy is retained only to test stale-version exclusion.
Under version 1.0, full-time employees received 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Carryover was limited to 3 days and expired on 28 February of the next year. Employees submitted leave requests at least 5 working days in advance.

## Replacement
This policy ceased to apply on 2025-12-31. Current annual leave rules are in NS-HR-001 version 2.0, effective 2026-01-01. Do not apply these archived values to current leave questions.
