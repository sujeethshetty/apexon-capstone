# Remote Work and Equipment

## Work location
Northstar Labs is a fictional company. Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with line-manager agreement. Teams choose their common office days. A fully remote arrangement requires a written exception approved by People Operations and the department head.

## Availability
Employees must be available during team collaboration hours of 10:00–15:00 in their assigned office time zone, excluding their normal meal break. Availability does not mean continuous meetings. Employees record planned remote days in TeamCalendar.

## Equipment and security
The one-time home-office equipment allowance is USD 300 per employee, available after the employee completes 30 calendar days of employment. Obtain line-manager approval before purchase and submit itemized receipts through SpendDesk. The allowance covers an external keyboard, mouse, monitor, or ergonomic desk accessories. Personal broadband subscriptions are not covered.
Use the company-managed laptop for company work and follow NS-IT-001 for multi-factor authentication and remote access. Remote work from another country requires prior review by People Operations; this policy does not specify permitted countries.
