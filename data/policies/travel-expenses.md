# Business Travel and Expenses

## Before travel
Northstar Labs is a fictional company. Business travel requires line-manager approval before booking. Trips with an estimated total cost above USD 1,500 also require Finance approval before booking. All monetary amounts in this policy are in USD.

## Booking and daily limits
Book economy-class flights. Hotel reimbursement is capped at USD 180 per night before tax. Meals are reimbursed at actual cost up to USD 60 per travel day, including tax and tips. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Unused daily meal allowance cannot be carried to another day.

## Claims and receipts
Submit the expense report in SpendDesk within 30 calendar days after the trip ends. Itemized receipts are required for any individual expense of USD 25 or more. A card statement alone is not an itemized receipt. Expenses below USD 25 still require a date, amount, business purpose, and vendor entry.

## Exceptions
Obtain Finance approval before incurring an expense above a stated cap. Approval of a trip does not automatically approve an above-cap hotel or meal. This policy does not authorize upgrades or personal-travel costs.
