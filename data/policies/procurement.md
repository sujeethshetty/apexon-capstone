# Procurement and Vendor Approval

## Purchase approvals
Northstar Labs is a fictional company. All new software purchases require a line-manager approval recorded in ProcureDesk. Purchases of USD 500 or more also require Finance approval. The USD 500 threshold applies to the total committed contract value, including recurring charges across the initial contract term, not just the monthly installment.

## Vendor review
Every new software vendor must pass IT Security review before purchase, even when the vendor offers a free trial. Vendors that will process personal data also require Privacy review before any personal data is shared. A manager's approval does not replace Security or Privacy review.

## Ordering and payment
Procurement issues a purchase order after the required approvals and reviews are complete. Do not use a personal credit card for a software subscription. Software renewals must be submitted in ProcureDesk at least 20 working days before the renewal date.

## Scope
Travel and home-office equipment reimbursements follow NS-FIN-001 and NS-HR-002 respectively. This document does not name approved vendors or provide spending authority to the knowledge assistant.
