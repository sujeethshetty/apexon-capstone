# Annual Leave Policy

## Scope and allowance
Northstar Labs is a fictional company. These policies are original demonstration data, not employment or legal advice.
Full-time employees receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated allowance based on contracted working days. Public holidays do not reduce the annual leave allowance.

## Requests and approval
Submit annual leave in PeopleDesk at least 10 working days before the first day away. The line manager approves requests after checking team coverage. Requests for more than 10 consecutive working days also require approval from the department head.

## Carryover and records
An employee may carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March of that next year. People Operations maintains the authoritative leave balance in PeopleDesk; this policy does not contain individual balances.
